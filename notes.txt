What your game code needs to support:
- Flipbooks: a sprite-sheet particle player that reads the grid layouts and effect_presets from assets/asset_manifest.json.
- Explosions through the hull: honour depth_test: false on ShipExplosion, ShipShockwave and LiftCoreSabotage, or the ship will hide the blast.
- Skies: these are flat panoramas with no dome mesh, so you need a panoramic skybox or sky dome shader.
- Socket matching: preset sockets are glob patterns where the first match wins, and hit_point and camera are anchors your code supplies at runtime.
